\subsection{Theorems for \GRFRP}
\label{sec:semantics:grfrp:theorems}

This section establishes the key semantic properties of \GRFRP services. While services operate in
an asynchronous environment, their correctness and predictability depend on the well-behaved nature
of the synchronous \GRSync components and the \GReact operations they orchestrate.

As established in \Cref{sec:semantics:grsync:theorems}, the causality of all \GRSync components is a
fundamental property, statically guaranteed by the compiler. This ensures that every component is
both productive and deterministic. This guarantee is the essential precondition for analyzing the
behavior of \GRFRP services.

Building on this foundation, we now prove that \GRFRP services are themselves well-behaved. We
establish the following key properties:
\begin{enumerate}
      \item \emph{Productivity}: A service always produces a valid state transition and a
            set of outputs in response to any incoming value
            (\Cref{thm:semantics:grfrp:theorems:productivity}).
      \item \emph{Determinism}: The sequence of outputs from a service is uniquely
            determined by the history of its inputs
            (\Cref{thm:semantics:grfrp:theorems:determinism}).
      \item \emph{Equivalence}: The push-based operational semantics is consistent with the
            stream-based denotational semantics (\Cref{thm:semantics:grfrp:theorems:equivalence}).
      \item \emph{Soundness}: The operational semantics is sound with respect to input
            oversampling (\Cref{thm:semantics:grfrp:theorems:oversampling}).
\end{enumerate}
